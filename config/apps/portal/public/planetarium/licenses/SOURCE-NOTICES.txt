Deep Star Maps 2020: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/
Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
Visualization: Ernie Wright (USRA).
Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC.
Catalogue sources include Hipparcos-2, Tycho-2, Gaia DR2, Yale Bright Star
Catalog, UCAC3 and XHIP. Constellation figures on that source page are based
on work for the IAU by Alan MacRobert, Roger Sinnott and Rick Fienberg;
those raster figure images are not shipped in this package.
Modified here: 4k linear HDR maps tone-mapped and encoded as compact sRGB JPEGs.

CGI Moon Kit: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
Color map: NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University.
Elevation map: NASA/GSFC/MIT.
Visualization: Ernie Wright (USRA).
Color uses the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC) composite;
elevation uses Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA) data.
Modified here: compact JPEG encoding; the distributed height map retains its
1024x512 source dimensions and is used as visual surface relief, not geodetic output.
