The exact user request is preserved in user-brief.txt. This is the second serious
research stage, not website phase2 or release phase9. Stage I remains immutable.

| Brief section | Evidence | Scope and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Stochastic one-bit feedback | src/stochastic.mjs, docs/SIMULATOR.md | Fair initial H/V; uniformly sampled anchors; controller observes only success/failure. Event acceleration uses geometry internally, never as policy input. |

| 3. Feedback vs temporal null | Frozen 400-test family; full frontier and all21 diagonal nulls | Additional pilot-fitted proposal-persistence nulls. | 4. Large policy41 systems | large_size config, 3072 runs, signed S/moments/quantiles/ECDF and paired effects | L128/256/512/1024/2048, k4/8. Finite-size trends measured; thermodynamic limit remains unresolved. No forced extrapolation or phase transition. |
| 5. Stochastic liveness | docs/THEORY.md, exact graph tests | Necessary and sufficient geometry-specific conditional-failure kernel criterion; universal abstract-availability criterion distinguished from actual empty-start RSA reachability. |
| 6. Analytic two-state family | Exact waiting kernel/moments, rare-excursions diagnostics | Beta>0 removes strict policy deadlock. Divergence depends on the state; legal-state rare excursions additionally show nonuniform integrability. Liveness is separate from speed and density. |
| 7. Exact small systems | 52 rational cases, 212992 validation runs, simultaneous99% joint-law audit | L2/3,k2, open/periodic,9 base and4 extra rational pairs. Exact numerator/denominator authoritative. Model checks do not establish large-system conclusions. |

| 9. Two-bit escalation | docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md | Deferred. This stage establishes and tests simple one-bit mechanisms; it does not enumerate a million larger controllers. |
| 10. Unexpected phenomena | Independent opposite-corner pilot and rare-excursion data | Accepted anti-correlation, coarse-winner regression, small exploration/long waits followed. No unmeasured metastability or spatial-domain statistics claimed. |
| 11. Success need not be positive | Final report and paper | Negative, limited and unresolved answers are retained; Stage I conclusion is not rewritten. |
| 12. Q1/Q2/Q3 | Final report and paper | Answered at the exact/finite-size/evaluated-family level supported by evidence. Mathematical limits of finite observations are explicit. |
