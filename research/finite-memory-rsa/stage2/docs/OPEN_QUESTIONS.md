# Open questions after Stage II

1. Does policy41 global bias eventually vanish at k8, or approach a small nonzero
   value after a slower crossover? L2048 remains substantially biased. Longer
   size ranges and independent high-n signed distributions are needed; the
   current decrease does not identify a thermodynamic limit.
2. What is the rate of decay of coverage differences? Current largest-size
   intervals cross zero, but failure to reject is not equivalence. A future
   equivalence margin must be justified and fixed before new measurements.
3. Can a one-bit controller outperform a densely resolved constrained temporal
   frontier? The finite21-point diagonal and a few matched points do not prove
   a universal positive or negative result over the continuum or arbitrary
   renewal/open-loop schedules.
4. Does feedback's reduction of accepted-direction imbalance control spatial
   domains or just the total H/V count? Pair correlations and domain sizes were
   not measured. Accepted run distributions do not answer the spatial question.
5. Would a geometry-independent renewal controller reproduce the same accepted
   dynamics and density? Matching proposal lag1 alone is not causal isolation
   from all higher-order temporal structure. A fair follow-up would preregister
   multiple matched temporal observables without supplying geometry to a policy.
6. Additional memory may maintain accepted balance while preserving short-range
   patterns. Before any two-bit search, reduce unreachable states, minimize,
   quotient state/HV symmetries and filter liveness. No brute-force escalation
   or next research stage is authorized by this delivery.
7. Rare-event-aware kinetic estimators are needed near beta0. Exact moments can
   demonstrate why sample-variance intervals fail; ordinary replication alone
   can miss excursions when n*beta is small. Physical-time and accepted-event
   interpretations should remain separate.
