# Open scientific questions

These are proposed follow-up studies, not results or promises of novelty.

1. **What is the large-system fate of policy41's anisotropy?** Its mean absolute
   orientation order decreases materially between L=16 and256. More sizes,
   adequate independent runs and preselected competing scaling laws are needed
   before interpreting a nonzero limit or a transition. Small commensurate tori
   must be separated from asymptotic behaviour.
2. **Does balanced deadlock lose a vanishing amount of coverage?** Policy38's
   deadlock probability increases with size while external rescue demonstrates
   residual capacity. Deadlock frequency and the size of its coverage cost are
   different observables. A follow-up should pair untouched terminal lattices
   with geometric completion and measure size-dependent gain distributions.
3. **Can stochastic failure-fair policies improve the constrained frontier?**
   The stochastic RRSA-like reference is not failure-fair. A small,
   symmetry-constrained probability grid with an independent holdout could
   separate feedback effects from orientation bias and open-loop correlations.
4. **Which spatial structures explain temporal persistence?** Connected empty
   components, local orientation correlations and domain sizes were not measured
   systematically here. An image is insufficient evidence of a domain law.
   Define these observables first and evaluate independent runs.
5. **What extra capability requires four states?** A million labelled two-bit
   controllers are not a million distinct behaviours. Extend rooted minimization
   and failure-cycle safety certificates before undertaking a staged search;
   reserve new seeds and account for candidate selection.
6. **How do noise and delay change safety?** Random feedback errors may destroy
   deterministic traps, but that does not automatically improve isotropic packing.
   The current event sampler assumes a deterministic failure path and cannot be
   reused unchanged for noisy/delayed state transitions.
7. **Can controller deadlock be classified exactly from an empty lattice?** The
   universal structural fairness certificate is sufficient, not necessary at
   each fixed L,k. Characterize physically reachable failure cycles rather than
   equating a graph defect with certain failure on every substrate.

Before expanding any search, obtain an external human review of the formal
model, exact solver, stopping conventions and statistical family. This draft
has internal computational and literature checks, not journal peer review.
