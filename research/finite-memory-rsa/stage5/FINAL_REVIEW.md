# Final adversarial review

This is an AI-assisted hostile self-review, not independent peer review.
Reasonable objections are retained, including ones not resolved by the work.
Supporting exact files are indexed by the final evidence and hash manifests.

| Objection | Evidence | Resolution | Remaining limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Outcome-dependent RSA is being advertised as a new invention. | RRSA primary prior art and [literature notes](docs/FINAL_LITERATURE.md). | Remove mechanism-level priority claims; describe audited matched-memory conclusions. | No exhaustive novelty review or external priority assessment. |
| A switching grid is weaker than the claimed complete stochastic null. | Two full four-entry row simplexes, both initial-state vertices, dyadic covering certificates. | Main family is complete ≤2-state edge-emitting HMM, including action/state correlation and boundaries. | Bounds do not recover its full attained region. |

| Absence of an outer-support crossing was relabelled inclusion. | Initial raw-membership calculation remains unresolved; later proof uses exact coordinate dominance. | Claim only monotone dominated temporal convex closure and all-nonnegative-direction support negativity periodically. | No raw-point inclusion, nonconvex equality or full randomized-feedback theorem. |

| AI involvement or missing human review is concealed. | Disclosure names formalization, code, calculations, writing and self-review assistance. | No invented human author, affiliation, publication or peer-review status. | All derivations warrant independent expert inspection. |

The final result is not a universal answer to whether a bit of feedback helps.
It is a proved deterministic-one-bit periodic support-negative theorem,
two exact tiny density suprema, and explicitly unresolved open and randomized
extensions. The project pauses with those limits rather than changing the
old conclusions or expanding the search until a desired answer appears.
