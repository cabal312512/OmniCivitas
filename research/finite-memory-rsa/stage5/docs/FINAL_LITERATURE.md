## Local contribution versus background

The defensible local contributions are the audited deterministic catalogues
and physical quotients, explicit randomized memory witnesses, exact RSA
joint laws, the periodic deterministic-one-bit support-negative certificate,
the tiny density supremum and model-specific residual support certificates.
The mixture and eventual-periodic approximation arguments are included as
explicit proofs using elementary probability; no broad priority claim is
made for those general principles.

The complete randomized-feedback capability question is not solved. The
new parameter-box method failed to beat the unrestricted word bound, and
the open joint supports remain too wide. These omissions are conclusions,
not details to conceal behind references. No affiliation, external peer
review or human author verification is invented. The main manuscript and
final review disclose the actual AI-assisted workflow.
