# Open questions and next-stage boundaries

The exact structure is complete, but the performance survey is not a global
true-expectation optimization over every four-state behavior at every lattice size.
No negative holdout establishes a thermodynamic impossibility theorem.

1. Can any pruned class yield a balanced, low-cost advantage at larger L? L16 means
   are noisy; fifty k8 selected classes were capped, and many other classes were
   pruned structurally by the performance selection. A new follow-up would need a
   new protocol and seed block, not an edit to this confirmation lock.
2. Does the exact one-bit cost/imbalance frontier extension persist beyond L3/k2,
   or under exact external mixtures and stochastic outcome-blind controllers?
   Current mixture diagnostics are numerical and exploratory.
3. Is there a useful general density upper bound for outcome-only finite memory?
   No coupling or asymptotic theorem currently proves one-bit insufficiency,
   two-bit superiority or a finite-memory no-go result.
4. Can a few well-identified deterministic mechanisms justify a low-dimensional
   stochastic relaxation? No unrestricted dozens-of-probabilities optimization
   or three-bit brute-force enlargement is authorized by the results alone.
5. Can state-dependent rare-event estimation be constructed when entry probability
   and conditional dwell law are unknown and occupancy changes after success?
   The demonstrated conditional estimator assumes both are known in a fixed model.
6. Can forest valuation computation scale without explicit forest enumeration?
   The current independent enumerator is capped at six states. Classic graph
   algorithms may support larger certificates; this is an implementation question.
7. A three-action geometric extension could test how the mixed-failurecycle
   criterion generalizes. It was optional and was not performed.

The locked primary analysis is a fixed-design research record, not a general-purpose
statistical library. Its hardcoded 768 repetitions and metadata assumptions are
checked by the separate row audit/reconstruction guard. A zero-SE nonzero-effect
branch would be unsuitable as automatic general inference; all 612 current tests
have positive SE and it was never executed. Future generalization requires a new
validated analysis version and lock, preserving this primary source and results.

The rare-draw implementation has finite PRNG resolution and a finite inverse-
geometric tail. Exact continuum moments/asymptotics come from rational algebra
and proofs, not extrapolating finite random samples.

Website integration, PDFs, and the full public release portability audit require
the user's next instruction. They were not silently begun here.
