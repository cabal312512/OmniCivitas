# What remains open after Stage IV

1. Does any ≤4-state feedback point lie outside the complete same-operational-
   memory temporal **joint** region in (θ,E|S|,E[A/N]) under a fixed liveness scope?
   No strict certified crossing was found. Not crossing an outer relaxation
   does not prove inclusion. Local search failure does not prove exclusion.
2. Can a certified positive-parameter subdivision close the full two-state edge-
   HMM frontier before attempting the 31-dimensional four-state family? Exact
   absorption rational functions are available, but singular proper boundaries
   need separate strata and cannot be discarded.
3. What is the general minimal **randomized feedback** realization of a physical
   process? Deterministic quotient minima are not enough. The explicit 2→3
   activation theorem is proved; most sampled feedback minima remain intervals.
4. Can the strong abstract-universal-liveness density supremum on periodic 3×3
   be characterized? H-once-V-forever proves the fixed-geometry-proper optimum
   only. Approaching a boundary policy and attaining it are different questions.
5. Can all-pairs minimum distinguishing geometries be computed efficiently,
   with parameterized ordering of k and boundary? Current BFS and constructive
   size guarantees are useful but do not produce that entire table.
6. Can positive forest leading-term computation avoid exponential forest
   enumeration for larger rare-event kernels while keeping tied coefficients?
7. Which measurable nested levels in actual RSA isolate unknown rare entrance
   without creating nearly extinct splitting cohorts? Product-model unbiasedness
   and scaling do not provide a useful automatic RSA level design.
8. Is there a finite-precision-correct sampler/estimator or rigorous numerical
   bias bound for extremely small dwell hazards? The benchmark uses ordinary
   32-bit uniforms/double geometric sampling and explicitly lacks that guarantee.

Do not resolve these by increasing controller memory or large-lattice Monte
Carlo without a theory-driven reason. Do not change or reselect Stage I–III
results. A future stage may target one concrete gap after user authorization.
